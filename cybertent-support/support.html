CyberTent Support

Updated November 18, 2026.

CyberTent provides full-screen nameplates, status cards, a focus timer, QR display and cropping, a teleprompter, ambience and moon blocks. It is an iPhone app, with English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese interfaces. Minimum installation version is iOS 17; this release's validation covers iOS 27 only. StandBy is managed by iOS.

Change the language

In iOS Settings, open Apps → CyberTent and choose the preferred language if available. The app also provides Settings → Open app language settings. Available languages are English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. New templates follow the app language. Existing names, cards, scripts and QR content stay as you wrote them.

Show a status card

Open Cards, choose a card and tap Display this card. In full screen, Switch scene lets you choose another card. Any active focus timer continues.

Add StandBy

First enable sharing in the card editor. On Duo, enter StandBy, touch and hold the clock face and unlock, then choose Modular → Customize → a widget area → CyberTent. On other iPhones, connect a charger, rest the locked phone on its side, and touch and hold the StandBy widget area to add CyberTent. The in-app guide is at Settings → Add CyberTent to StandBy. Layout and refresh are controlled by iOS.

QR code missing from the widget

Edit a QR or social card, enable Share text in StandBy and Also share the QR code, then save and select it. Importing an image alone does not authorize sharing. Night colors and brightness may affect scanning. Use full-screen display in the app when needed and preserve the white border.

Hide widget content immediately

Turning sharing off updates the saved snapshot, but the system may temporarily retain the old view. Remove the widget from the Home Screen or StandBy to hide it immediately.

Restore a cropped image

The source image is unchanged. Cropping again starts from the saved crop. Import the original image again to recover areas previously cropped out.

Missing focus reminder

Check Settings → Focus reminders and system notification permission. Focus modes, sound settings and other system controls can affect how reminders appear. Also check the actual timer state.

Where is my data?

Content stays on your device. There is no account or app-operated cloud sync. Backups follow system settings. When troubleshooting, update without uninstalling to preserve local content.

Privacy

Read the full policy offline in Settings → Privacy policy, or visit the privacy page on this website.

Find full-screen controls

Controls hide after 5 idle seconds by default. Tap the canvas or the ellipsis to show them. Disable auto-hide in Settings if preferred. With touch lock enabled, the unlock hint stays hidden until you touch the screen, then disappears after a few seconds. Press and hold for 2 seconds to unlock; VoiceOver provides an Unlock controls button. Single-card full screen is the default. Enable Remember display layout to keep an explicitly selected two-way layout.

Organize cards

Long-press to favorite a card, or open Manage to favorite and drag cards into order. Favorites come first. Cards and the full-screen switcher use the same order. Recently displayed only tracks cards actually opened in full screen.

Back up and restore

Open Settings → Backup and restore to export a complete .duotentbackup file or preview an imported file. Merge adds cards, focus history and moon-block history while keeping your current scripts, timer and settings. Replace restores everything. A local recovery copy is kept before restoring and can be previewed again. Imported cards are not automatically shared. A running timer in a replacement backup is paused at the backup time; resume it manually. Reminders are disabled.

Energy settings

Continuous pet animation is reduced in Low Power Mode or below 20% battery, or whenever you turn the option on. Optionally dim after inactivity in full screen. Touching the screen, leaving display or backgrounding restores brightness, unless you changed it manually. No fixed battery-saving percentage is promised.

Independent widgets and backups

Touch and hold an added widget, then edit it to follow the current display, show a fixed public card, or show focus status. Fixed cards remain independent of tools in the app. Logo sharing requires separate permission. iOS manages widget choices: app backups include cards, IDs, typography and images, but do not export or automatically restore system widget positions or configurations. Check card selections and sharing permissions after restoring.

Celebration cards

Choose a birthday, coffee, or gift template under New. Edit your message and signature, save, and open full screen. Place your phone alongside the real cake, coffee or gift for a photo.

Focus and rest

Open Tools → Focus timer. Choose 1–120 focus minutes, 1–60 rest minutes and a long rest after 1–8 sessions. By default, each stage waits for you to choose what comes next. Auto-advance is optional: a finite flow ends after the long rest, and uses saved timestamps even while the app is closed. Do not enable it if you do not want time away to count. Pausing excludes that time; ending early saves an incomplete record. Completed sessions count on the local calendar day they end. Today’s totals exclude breaks and early finishes. Recent history shows the latest 30 records; backups include up to 10,000 records. Clear history in Recent history without stopping a current timer.

Choose a return card before starting. Ending the flow returns there only if you have not changed scenes; otherwise use Return to previous card when you want. Deleting that card falls back to the current available card. Changing cards or pets never resets the timer.

Focus with your pet uses your cat or dog from Ambience, including the Show pet switch. Linked pets stay quiet while focusing, stretch when paused and show a happy completion pose. Taps take priority for 3 seconds. Turning the link off restores your fixed or automatic expression setting. A completed session does not replay a celebration on reopening.

Notifications and StandBy use the same finite timeline. Pausing, resuming or ending replaces pending reminders. System notification settings and widget refresh policies still apply. A replacement backup restore pauses the flow at backup time and disables reminders and public focus sharing; merging retains your current timer and deduplicates focus history.

Live Activities

Enable Focus Live Activity in Tools → Focus timer. It shows a generic stage and countdown, without personal card content. Pause, resume and end in the app update it. If removed, it stays removed until you choose Show Live Activity again or explicitly start the next stage. If iOS denies or ends it, your timer continues. Live Activities use a system countdown, not a background per-second timer. When a stage expires while the app is suspended, it shows that the stage has ended and asks you to open the app to sync; an automatic flow and its local reminders still follow saved dates. iOS controls availability, placement and lifetime; no full-screen StandBy takeover is promised.

Quick start

In Settings → Quick start, open Shortcuts and find CyberTent (赛博小帐篷 in Simplified Chinese, 賽博小帳篷 in Traditional Chinese). Choose Show a selected card, Start a focus timer, or Open pet desktop. Card and minute parameters are editable; actions require unlocking and open the app. Starting while another flow exists opens that flow without restarting it, including paused or completed stages. End the flow before starting a different duration. A deleted card must be reselected. Pet desktop shows your chosen companion without resetting focus. App backups do not include system shortcuts or live-activity permissions.

Multiple scripts and quick pause

Create scripts in Tools → Teleprompter. Use each script's menu to rename it (edit its title), duplicate or delete it. Each keeps its own font size, speed, mirroring, highlight, opening countdown and text-based reading position. At least one script remains. Tap the text, use the persistent pause button or press Space on a keyboard to pause or resume. Jump directly to a paragraph. Backgrounding, scene switching, width changes and touch lock pause playback. Editing the body resets progress; title or font changes keep your place. The opening countdown applies when starting from the beginning. The compact translucent control bar keeps more of the script visible. Tap the speed number to adjust speed in a popover; opening it pauses playback, and closing it does not automatically resume. Enable Reduce Transparency in iOS to use a solid control background.

Personalize your companion

Give cats and dogs separate names, choose from three coats each, add a bandana or bow and select an activity. Each species has its own rhythm. Reduce Motion and energy saving stop automatic changes and keep a still pose. Focus companionship and touch reactions take priority.

Share card templates

Use the share button above a card preview or its context menu. The default keeps the style and uses fictional examples. Individually choose any original title, organization, signature, QR text, QR image or logo to include. Check the preview and text before exporting a .duotenttemplate file or a 1600×900 preview image; share the saved file using Files. Import card template below your card list previews and adds a new card without replacing existing cards, timers or scripts. It stays private until you allow StandBy sharing. Repeated imports create separate cards.

Language and backup scope

English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese follow the language selected for this app in iOS. Saved content is never translated. Full backups include all scripts and their reading positions, plus both pets' names and appearance. Replace restores these settings; merge keeps your local scripts and settings. Language selection, like notifications and other system settings, is managed by iOS and is not overwritten by backups.
