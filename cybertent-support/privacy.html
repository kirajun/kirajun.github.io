CyberTent Privacy Policy

Applies to version 1.0.0. Effective November 18, 2026.

Developer and contact

林峻 (Jun Lin) — kirajun@gmail.com
CyberTent is named 赛博小帐篷 in Simplified Chinese and 賽博小帳篷 in Traditional Chinese.

Content on your device

CyberTent has no account registration, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK or developer-operated cloud service. The app does not upload cards, images, scripts, focus status, focus history or moon-block history to the developer or third-party servers.

Cards, settings, scripts, focus state, up to 10,000 recent focus records and the latest 30 moon-block results are stored locally. The app accesses only the images you choose in the system Photos or Files picker. It does not browse your photo library or request camera, microphone, contacts or location permission. QR detection, orientation correction and confirmed cropping happen on device. Imported logos are oriented locally. A processed PNG is saved without changing the source image or copying its location metadata. Processing limits the longest edge to 4,096 pixels. Cancelling a crop does not replace the saved image.

Display and widgets

All card text and stable card identifiers, and focus status, that you allow to be shared, plus the current result when showing moon blocks, may be written to a local shared container for the CyberTent widget. Scripts and focus history are never included.

QR sharing is off by default. Only after you enable both Share text in StandBy and Also share the QR code and save the card does the app write a 600 × 600 QR preview with a white border alongside the shared text. Original image files and QR source strings are not copied into the snapshot, but the preview remains scannable and contains the same information. People nearby may see or scan locked-screen content. Share only what you want others to see.

Logos are private by default. A separate logo-sharing switch permits a preview no larger than 240 pixels on its longest edge. Each widget may follow the current display, show a fixed public card, or show focus status. Fixed cards remain independent when you switch cards or tools in the app. Revoking sharing or deleting a card hides that content the next time the public index is read. iOS may keep an already rendered widget view temporarily. Remove the widget from the Home Screen or StandBy to hide it immediately. The app does not automatically visit URLs contained in QR codes.

Notifications

Notification permission is requested only if you enable focus and rest reminders. These are local notifications. You can disable reminders in the app or revoke permission in system Settings. Other features work without notification permission.

Retention, deletion and backups

You can edit or delete cards, replace images, edit scripts, clear focus or moon-block history and turn off public sharing. Saving media changes or deleting cards removes imported files that are no longer referenced. Unused files left by cancelled editing or interruption are cleaned up on the next successful load. Removing the app removes its local app data; system widget caches and device backups are managed by iOS. Backups may include local content depending on your device settings. You can export a complete, unencrypted backup using the system Files picker. It contains cards, images, scripts, settings and local history. Store it somewhere you trust. Your chosen file provider may sync it according to its settings; CyberTent does not automatically upload backups. Before restoring, the app keeps a recovery copy in its private local directory. The next restore replaces that copy; uninstalling removes it with app data. Imported cards have public, QR and logo sharing disabled. A replacement restore also disables reminders and pauses any running timer at the backup time. There is no app-operated cloud sync.

Contacting support

The feedback entry prepares an editable email draft with the app version, Build, iOS version and device type. It includes no cards, images or scripts and never sends automatically. If system Mail is not configured, you can copy the address and feedback details.

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. They are used to handle your request and reply. Do not send passwords, private QR codes or unrelated sensitive information. Contact the same address to request deletion of personal information in support emails. You control local app content on your device; the developer cannot access or delete it remotely.

Policy updates

If features or data handling change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated. Questions: kirajun@gmail.com.

Support website

This website serves static help and policy pages, without advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms. The hosting provider processes routine network request information, such as your IP address, needed to serve the pages. This is separate from content stored in the offline app.

Live Activities and Shortcuts

Focus Live Activities are off by default. When enabled, they pass only the timer stage, time and an internal flow identifier to the system for display on supported Lock Screens and Dynamic Islands. They do not include card names, text, QR codes, logos, scripts or history. People nearby may see the timer. You can turn this feature off in the app or in system Settings. Live Activity preferences and identifiers are device-specific and are not exported in app backups; a backup restore disables this feature.

Shortcuts can list your local card names and identifiers so you can choose a card. Their three actions require unlocking and open the app. They do not automatically grant public widget access. Shortcuts saved or synced by Apple follow your system settings; CyberTent does not operate a shortcut or Siri server. There is no automatic Spotlight indexing of private cards.

Personalization, scripts and template sharing

Pet names, coats, accessories, scripts and reading positions stay in private local storage and full backups you explicitly export. They are not added to the public widget index. Language is managed by iOS; changing it does not translate saved content.

Template sharing is initiated by you and uses fictional examples by default. Original text, QR content, images and logos enter the export only when individually selected. A preview is shown before export. Editable template files and preview images do not include other cards, scripts or focus history. Imported templates create new cards with public sharing disabled. A file provider you select may sync or share exported content according to its settings. The app never sends it to anyone automatically.

Images inside imported template files retain their file content, including any embedded metadata. The photo-import metadata removal described above does not run again for template files. Only include images you intend to share.

GitHub Pages

This public support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes network request information such as IP addresses to operate and protect the hosting service. These website requests are separate from content stored locally by the app.
